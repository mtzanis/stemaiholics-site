# My Voice & Story

## Why equity matters to me

I imagine a world where all students can be equipped with all these STEM skills that will make them tackle the 21st century challenges with great success. It shouldn't really matter what language you speak. It shouldn't really matter what country you're coming from. Everyone should have equal access to STEM education. Because through STEM, I strongly believe that you can be successful in life. You can develop critical thinking. It helps you with your imagination. And I won't stop believing that a world with more STEM, with more hands-on experiences for students, is a better world.
